At AI First Dayananda Sagar University, we believe the best way to learn AI is to build with it. That's why we're excited to introduce our **AI Factory**, a high-performance computing facility built on 20 NVIDIA DGX B200 systems, paired with an **AI Maker Space** where ideas turn into working prototypes.

## What is an AI Factory?

A traditional factory takes in raw materials and produces finished goods. An AI factory takes in data and compute and produces intelligence: trained models, fine-tuned assistants, and AI-powered applications. It gives our students and researchers the infrastructure to train, fine-tune, and deploy AI at a scale that was once available only to large tech companies.

## The Hardware: 20 DGX B200 Systems

Each NVIDIA DGX B200 is built around eight NVIDIA Blackwell B200 GPUs connected through fifth-generation NVLink. Together, our 20 systems give us:

- **160 Blackwell B200 GPUs**
- **About 28.8 TB of total GPU memory** (1,440 GB per system)
- **Roughly 1.4 exaFLOPS of FP8 training performance** (72 petaFLOPS per system)
- **Roughly 2.9 exaFLOPS of FP4 inference performance** (144 petaFLOPS per system)

That's enough capacity to train and fine-tune large language models, run computer vision and multimodal research, and serve AI applications to many users at once.

## The Software: CUDA Platform

The hardware is powered by NVIDIA's CUDA platform, the foundation of modern GPU computing. CUDA and its ecosystem (cuDNN, TensorRT, NCCL and more) let frameworks like PyTorch, TensorFlow, and JAX run efficiently on our GPUs. For students, this means hands-on experience with the same tools used across industry and research labs worldwide.

## The AI Maker Space

Compute alone doesn't create innovation; people do. The AI Maker Space is a collaborative area where students, faculty, and researchers can:

- Experiment with ideas and build prototypes
- Work on interdisciplinary projects across engineering, healthcare, agriculture, and more
- Learn through workshops, hackathons, and mentoring
- Turn classroom concepts into real-world AI solutions
